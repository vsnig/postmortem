# Chrome Web Store listing

**Name:** Postmortem — stop the "one more game" reflex

**Short description (132/132):** Blocks Rematch / New Game on Lichess and Chess.com for 15 min, so you analyze the game you just played instead of tilting into more.

**Long description:**

You finish a chess game, see a new opening idea or a blunder worth understanding, and before you think about it you have clicked "Rematch" or "New 3 min". Ten games later the idea is gone, you are on tilt, and your rating is lower than when you started.

Postmortem breaks that reflex. One click on the toolbar icon and, for the next 15 minutes, the one-click paths into a new game on Lichess and Chess.com are switched off: Rematch, New opponent, New game, Play again, and the lobby quick-pairing pane. When a game ends, a short "Game over. Postmortem time." overlay covers the buttons for five seconds and offers the site's own analysis board instead. Click the icon again to unblock.

It is not a wall. You can still type a URL and play. It only removes the automatic click, so the decision to play another game is a decision again.

Use it to:
- stop playing "one more game" on Chess.com or Lichess when you are tilting
- actually analyze your chess games instead of queueing the next one
- explore an opening you just met while it is still fresh
- put a cooldown on blitz and bullet chess without blocking the sites entirely

Works on lichess.org and chess.com. No account, no data collection, nothing leaves your browser.
